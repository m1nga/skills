---
name: study-evidence-packet
description: Assemble a private evidence packet after a study-abroad administrator requests additional documents. Trace study history, match records to the notice, draft missing items and reconcile files from another device.
---

Start with the actual notice and the applicant's authorized sources. Keep case files in the applicant's private workspace. If the notice is unavailable, prepare an inventory and mark the requirements provisional until its wording is available.

1. Transcribe each requested item and any explicit deadline, time zone, format, language, delivery route or signing requirement. Preserve ambiguous wording for clarification. Give each item a stable reference. Use the notice or a verified clarification for case-specific requirements.
2. Inventory the approved folders, exports and mailbox scope. Record which were available, the search terms and date range where applicable, and unreadable or unavailable sources. Preserve original attachments, sender and message identifiers. Give each source an ID, a private locator and, for file copies, a SHA-256 digest. Byte-identical copies can share a content reference while retaining distinct provenance. Preserve conflicting versions. Work on separate copies and keep signed originals intact, including embedded signatures and validation information.
3. Reconstruct the study timeline. For each event record the supported date or interval, institution, what occurred, and a source page, passage or message. Distinguish offer, registration, attendance, assessment, transfer and award. Record the event date separately from the document's issue or email date. Keep unresolved dates and conflicting accounts visible. For scans use OCR for discovery and confirm consequential fields on the page image.
4. Match each requirement to evidence. For each candidate give the issuer, source kind, what the source establishes and what still needs confirmation. A receiving institution's transfer summary may establish accepted credit. To report original grades, locate the awarding institution's record or identify the missing evidence. A general teaching policy establishes available arrangements. To describe this applicant's actual study mode or location, use a record tied to the person, course and period, or label the account as self-reported. Distinguish originals, copies, translations, applicant explanations and drafts. Report signature verification only when actually performed.
5. Prepare a short response index in notice order, the cited timeline, the requested supporting files and a private action list. Keep internal evidence notes and unrequested sensitive records outside the candidate submission set. On each open item name the person who can resolve it, the exact missing field or document, a practicable next action and any stated due date. Label unsigned drafts clearly and leave unknown fields as visible placeholders. Draft an applicant explanation or an issuer request for the appropriate speaker. An issuer confirmation belongs to the issuer to verify and sign. Never transplant seals or signatures. For generated documents inspect the final rendered pages for clipped text, placeholders and missing attachments. Keep attachment names and index entries aligned.
6. When another device becomes available, compare its authorized sources against the existing inventory using content hashes and message/attachment identities. Distinguish unchanged, renamed, new, changed, unreadable and unavailable records. Revisit only affected claims, open items and outputs. An absent copy on one device does not establish deletion. Leave earlier originals and receipts intact. Report work finished now, the remaining dependencies and the evidence needed to close them. A prepared packet, a sent submission and an administrator's acceptance are separate states. External contact or submission needs its own authorization.
