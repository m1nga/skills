# Drink recipes

All recipes assume the espresso or pour-over parameters set in Step 2 of SKILL.md. This
file only covers "how to build the cup". Hardware specifics (basket size, steam wand,
grinder ranges) come from `~/.coffee/hardware.md`.

## Espresso (the base)

Dose per the registered basket (a common double basket: 17–19g); grind/temp/ratio/time
from the espresso baseline table in SKILL.md by roast level. This is the base for all
americanos and milk drinks.

## Hot americano
- 1 shot (double) + hot water
- Strength (espresso : water): standard 1:4–6, strong 1:3–4
- Water first, then espresso on top preserves crema; espresso first, water after blends
  more — user's preference

## Iced americano
- Fill the cup with ice
- Pour the espresso **straight onto the ice** (Japanese flash-chill — locks the
  aromatics; light roasts are brightest cold)
- Add cold water to taste; for "ice plus barely any water", go easy — melting ice will
  dilute further, so under-water it

## Big-batch strong (e.g. 1L)
- Multiple shots diluted to target volume
- Example: 1L strong ≈ **5 double shots (~215ml) + ~785ml water** (≈1:3.6 — properly
  strong)
- ⚠️ State **bean usage**: 5 doubles ≈ 85–95g of beans — roughly half a bag
- ⚠️ State **caffeine**: 5 doubles ≈ ~600mg, past the ~400mg/day guideline — don't drink
  it in one go
- Single-boiler machines: pause 20–30s every 2 shots to recover (per the machine notes in
  `hardware.md`); since it's all getting diluted, tiny per-shot temp drift barely matters
- Iced version: cold water + ice; hot version: hot water; light roasts read cleaner cold

## Latte / flat white
- Base: 1 double shot
- Steam milk to **microfoam** — smooth, faintly sweet, no big bubbles (per the milk setup
  in `hardware.md`; no steam wand → a hand frother or hot milk shaken in a jar is the
  honest fallback, texture will be simpler)
- Milk amount: flat white espresso:milk ≈ 1:3–4; latte more milk; cortado/piccolo by cup
  size
- Roast matching: light-roast espresso runs acidic — use slightly less milk to keep the
  coffee forward; medium-dark melts into milk and sweetens — right for big milk drinks
- Want a light roast that still stands up to milk: pull the ratio down to ~1:2 for a more
  concentrated shot

## V60 pour-over
- Dose: 15g (scale as needed, ratio 1:15–16)
- Grind: pour-over range (see `hardware.md` for dial numbers; otherwise medium-fine),
  nudged by roast — lighter finer, darker coarser
- Water temp: pour-over baseline table in SKILL.md
- Steps:
  1. Bloom: pour 2× the coffee weight in water (~30g), wait 30–45s for degassing (longer
     for fresh beans)
  2. 2–3 staged pours, steady spirals, up to target water weight
  3. Total time ~2:00–2:45; draining too fast → grind finer, too slow → coarser
- Light roasts: finer, hotter, longer. Dark roasts: coarser, cooler, shorter.
